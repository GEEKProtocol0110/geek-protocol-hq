Play your way

Host plays White in chess or Gold in the other games and moves first. The accepted opponent plays Black or Cyan. Only one request is accepted per room. You can resign or offer a draw; a rematch starts in a new lobby.

Chess includes castling, en passant and your selected promotion piece. Chess rules draw automatically on stalemate, repetition, fifty moves or insufficient material. Checkers requires captures and continued jumps, with crowning ending the turn. New strategy games show their rules with the board. Battle Geek fleet layouts stay private to each player. Both games have a 600-action practice limit. Online games have no undo.
