How to play
  1. Two players share this device. Gold (G) starts at the bottom and moves first; Cyan (C) starts at the top.
  2. Select a piece, then a marked dark square. Regular pieces move one diagonal step forward.
  3. If any capture is possible, you must jump. Continue a multiple jump with the same piece.
  4. Reach the far row to become a king (♛). Crowning ends that turn. Kings move and jump diagonally in either direction.
  5. Win when your opponent has no pieces or legal moves. Casual draws require both players to agree; automatic tournament draw claims are not implemented.

Use Tab to reach controls and Enter or Space to activate. Undo restores the previous move; it is for learning, not ranked competition.

Practice planning and recognizing patterns. These are games, not an IQ test.
