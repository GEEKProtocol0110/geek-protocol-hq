How to play
  1. Draw-one Klondike: seven columns, one face-up card per column, and a stock of 24 cards.
  2. Select a face-up card or descending run, then choose a column. Columns build down in alternating colors. Only a king or a run starting with a king can enter an empty column.
  3. Use the Foundation buttons to build four same-suit piles from ace to king. A foundation’s top card may move back to a column.
  4. Draw turns one stock card onto the waste. Only the top waste card is playable. Recycle the waste when the stock is empty; redeals are unlimited.
  5. Exposed face-down cards flip automatically. Win by placing all 52 cards on foundations. Some deals cannot be solved; undo or start a new deal whenever you like.

Use Tab to reach controls and Enter or Space to activate. Undo restores the previous move; it is for learning, not ranked competition.

Practice planning and recognizing patterns. These are games, not an IQ test.
