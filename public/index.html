Our storyA father, a son and a stack of cards.
WHERE IT BEGAN

A father. A son.
A stack of cards.

Make learning feel worth doing.

Geek Protocol began with a father making index cards to help his son learn to read. A word, a picture, a conversation. Something simple they could do together.

That idea grew into Proof-of-Learning: a place to explore, test your understanding, share what you know, and see your progress. The tools have grown. The reason is still the same.

From a child's first words to an adult's next discovery, we want curiosity to keep growing.

CLEAR BY DESIGN

What is live?

The learning path is live in Alpha. Kaspa 101, guided Study, untimed practice, and saved concept feedback are ready to use. Timed games track scores, XP, and internal game credits separately. Those credits cannot be withdrawn; on-chain reward settlement is not live.

Challenges, the daily vault, collectibles, and minting are available through Explore. GEEK KRC-20 minting is a separate, wallet-approved flow that pauses if live supply cannot be verified. Geek Protocol is an independent Kaspa community project.
