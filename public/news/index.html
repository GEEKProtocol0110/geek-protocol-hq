About these feeds & data timing

CoinGecko supplies the native top-100 crypto ranking; TradingView supplies the other embedded market data and publisher headlines. The top-100 snapshot is cached for up to five minutes; its fetch time is shown separately from provider quote freshness. CoinGecko ranks can include stablecoins and wrapped assets. Search filters the supplied 100 coins, not the entire crypto market. Quotes can be real-time, delayed or unavailable depending on the exchange, instrument and provider coverage. Follow the timestamps and notices inside the feed. Geek Protocol does not verify each quote or headline, and loading a frame does not establish that its data is current.

One selected feed loads at a time. Selecting Top 100 requests a public market snapshot from Geek Protocol; its server contacts CoinGecko without account or wallet information. There is no automatic background refresh. Pausing or switching views cancels the browser request and removes the table or frame. Pause removes it; Resume reconnects. Loading a feed connects your browser to TradingView, whose privacy practices apply. This page adds no AI-generated reporting or AI subscription. Provider availability and terms may change.
