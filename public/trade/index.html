External market · Wallet approval required
01 / Buy GEEK

Find an offer.

  1. Open GEEK’s Kaspacom page and choose Buy.
  2. Connect a wallet supported by Kaspacom on Kaspa Mainnet.
  3. Review the GEEK quantity, unit price, total KAS cost and fees.
  4. Choose an available order and review the final transaction in your wallet before approving.
02 / Sell GEEK

Set your listing.

  1. Open GEEK’s Kaspacom page and choose Sell.
  2. Connect the supported wallet that holds your on-chain GEEK.
  3. Enter the GEEK quantity and your asking price in KAS.
  4. Review the listing and wallet request. A listing needs a buyer; posting it does not guarantee a sale.
Before you approve

Know which GEEK you’re using.

On-chain GEEK: the GEEK KRC-20 token in your wallet is the asset used on the market.

HQ game credits: Alpha game credits, XP and collectibles are separate. They cannot be withdrawn or sold as GEEK tokens.

Minting and buying are different: one mint requests 100,000 GEEK for a 1 KAS protocol fee plus network fees. A marketplace order has a seller’s price and its own fees. Check your wallet’s final total.

HQ provides the market link and this guide. Orders, wallet connection and settlement happen on Kaspacom. Availability depends on the market and its indexer; no quote or liquidity is guaranteed here.
