Kaspa Live Widget

Bring Kaspa to your home screen with a neon price chart behind glass, KAS price, DAA score and observed network readings.

On Android, install the signed app and choose your currency, colors and widget size. On iPhone, use the free Scriptable app and copy our widget into it. No account or wallet required.

Set up for Android → Set up for iPhone →

The Android app is available as a signed APK. The iPhone Scriptable version is an early release awaiting a real iPhone trial.
