Kaspa Live Widget

Bring Kaspa to your home screen with a neon price chart behind frosted glass. Choose the compact 2×2 widget or the expanded 4×4 view, with time, KAS price and DAA score. The expanded view adds observed BPS and network hashrate.

Pick USD, EUR or GBP, dark or light appearance, and a 15-, 30- or 60-minute refresh interval. Cached readings stay visible with their age when a feed is unavailable.

Public release available: download the signed Android APK directly. No GitHub account required. Future signed releases update the same app and keep your settings and widgets.

Download the Android APK ↗ Release notes & checksum ↗

Install on Android

  1. On your Android phone, use Download the Android APK above to save kaspa-live-widget.apk.
  2. Open the APK on your Android phone. When prompted, allow that browser or file manager to install it.
  3. Open Kaspa Live Widget, choose your settings, then tap Save & add. Or long-press the home screen → Widgets → add Kaspa 2×2 or Kaspa 4×4.

Already using a debug test build? The public release installs as a separate app. Add its widgets, then remove the old test app when you are ready.

Reads public network data. No wallet connection, seed phrase or private key needed. Android may delay scheduled background updates; tap the widget to refresh.
