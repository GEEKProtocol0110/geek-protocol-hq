    Read the Royale rules
    1. The host plays too. The host chooses a limit from 2 to 100 players. At least two players must join; everyone in the room must be ready and online before the host starts. The room does not need to be full. The roster then locks.
    2. A five-second countdown starts the event. Every question has 15 seconds, followed by five seconds to reveal the answer and see who remains. The clock continues while you are offline.
    3. Your first answer locks. Wrong or missing answers eliminate you. Among correct answers, the slowest response-time group is also out unless everyone has exactly the same response time. A single correct survivor wins.
    4. Response time is measured when the server receives your answer, so network delay matters. Exact ties stay together; all-equal groups continue. Up to 100 distinct questions are available. Remaining players share victory if the question limit is reached. If everyone misses, the event has no winner.
    5. Reloading or recovering the same verified identity keeps your seat. Return before the current question’s deadline. Eliminated players may stay and watch; they cannot answer again.
    6. The host can remove waiting seats or end the event. An ended event has no winner. Invitations and results expire 24 hours after creation. The room-result medal is a visual keepsake here, not a transferable asset or proof of certified knowledge.
