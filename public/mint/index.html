A LITTLE HELP

Before you mint.

All hope, no hype. Know what your wallet is approving.

Can I mint from my phone or another wallet?

This page uses Kasware's KRC-20 mint API. Use a browser where Kasware is installed and available. An address alone, or a wallet used for player sign-in, does not provide this transaction capability. Check Kasware's supported options ↗

How much KAS do I need?

Each mint has a published 1 KAS protocol fee, plus commit and reveal network fees. Keep enough KAS for the total shown by Kasware. HQ requests no added priority fee; review the wallet's final total.

What happens if mint status is unavailable?

The page pauses minting and clears live totals until the indexer responds with a valid official record. It checks again automatically while visible, without requesting a wallet transaction. You can also refresh status yourself.

My mint was submitted. Where is my GEEK?

Open the reveal link in your receipt and check wallet activity. The network or indexer may take time to show the result. If there is an error or uncertain outcome, inspect both transactions before resetting the request. This page does not retry transactions automatically.

What does this mint give me?

A successful mint creates GEEK KRC-20 tokens for your wallet. It is separate from internal Alpha game credits and the planned 500-Geek NFT collection. It does not activate game withdrawals or guarantee value. Gross mints count toward the fixed 144 billion GEEK cap; burning does not reopen capacity.

How is the official mint checked?

Every fresh status must match the pinned ticker, supply, limit, decimals, deployer and deployment hash. Kasware presents the request for your approval. This interface has not completed an independent audit. Verify the complete request in your wallet; transactions are irreversible after broadcast.
