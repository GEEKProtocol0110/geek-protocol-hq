      GAUNTLET RULES

      Climb or cash out.

      1. Round 1 is free. Later rounds charge Alpha GEEK when you enter.
      2. Answer ten questions. Every round uses your selected category, a 15-second timer, and one locked answer.
      3. Build a streak. Consecutive correct answers add a growing score bonus.

      5. Choose after every round. Continue and risk another entry fee, or end the run.
