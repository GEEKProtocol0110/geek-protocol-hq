What the source labels mean

Source-reviewed items have been checked internally against their cited primary source. Draft imports still need an individual GEEK review of their facts, wording, category, and difficulty. Automatic duplicate checks do not prove an answer correct.

Kaspa includes 750 distinct source-checked questions. Study offers 537 concepts across eleven guided topics: Kaspa and practical technology. Each source-checked question carries its own review date; time-sensitive facts should be read in that context.

Build a bank worth learning from.

Before CCE reopens, every category needs 1,000 distinct questions with a checked source, a clear answer and a useful explanation. Draft imports and retired variants do not count toward this goal.
