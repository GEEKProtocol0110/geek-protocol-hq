Privacy notice

Effective November 19, 2026. This notice covers Geek Protocol HQ at www.geekprotocol.xyz. Geek Protocol is an independent Kaspa community project in public Alpha. Companion sites and apps (Geek Mini, Geek Jr, Geek Wallet and the Kaspa Live Widget) are covered by their own pages.

The short version

  • You can learn and play without an account, a wallet, an email address or a real name. We never ask for one.
  • We never request, receive or store a seed phrase, recovery phrase or private key. Nobody from Geek Protocol will ever ask for one.
  • We do not sell personal information and do not run advertising.
  • Traffic statistics are aggregate and skip visitors whose browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

What we store when you play

  • Session cookie. One HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax cookie holds a random session identifier for up to 30 days. It keeps your guest or signed-in progress together.
  • Player record. Your display name, Career XP, Alpha game credits, challenge, Study, Quest, vault and appearance progress are stored in our database so you can return to them. Alpha credits are internal and cannot be withdrawn.
  • Passkeys. If you create a passkey, we store its public key, credential ID, usage counter, the label you choose and its creation time. Fingerprint, face and screen-lock data stay on your device; the site never receives them or the passkey's private key.
  • Linked wallet. If you link a Kaspa wallet for account recovery, we store its public key and derived Mainnet address. We do not keep the signature you approve, and linking never requests a payment or a transaction.
  • Receiving addresses. If you save a receiving address on Wallet & rewards, it is stored with your account. Mainnet payouts are disabled; approved test rewards use Testnet-10.
  • Games and rooms. Multiplayer rooms store the seated players' display names, moves and results. Unaccepted invitations expire within 24 hours to 30 days; accepted games are kept until they finish. If you turn on turn alerts, we store that browser's push subscription (at most three devices per player).
  • Contributions. The contribution form collects a public display name, an optional public profile link and your description. Pending applications are kept up to 90 days, closed ones for 30 days. Public credit appears only with your consent and can be withdrawn.

What becomes public

Leaderboards, live rooms and Trivia Royale show your chosen display name and results. Hall of Thanks credits appear only after you request them. Pick a display name that does not identify you if you prefer to stay anonymous. Wallet addresses and transactions you broadcast to Kaspa are public on the blockchain by design.

Stored only in your browser

Some features keep data in your browser's local storage rather than on our servers: display preferences, saved practice runs, a signed-in hint, and your recent mint requests (up to 100 public addresses and transaction references) on Mint. Clear them with your browser's site-data controls.

Traffic statistics

Public pages use Vercel Web Analytics for aggregate statistics: pages viewed, referral sources, countries and device types. A few fixed learning events (for example, finishing a practice set) are counted by category only. Events never include names, wallet addresses, player IDs, answers, scores or form contents, and URLs are sent without query strings or fragments. The analytics service does not use third-party cookies. See Security for details.

Abuse protection

To limit spam and automated abuse, the server reads your IP address (IPv6 addresses by /64 prefix) and, for some forms, a network and browser fingerprint, and keeps short-lived request counters. These are not used for tracking or profiling.

Optional AI explanations

If the Study page offers an AI explanation and you tick its opt-in box, only your question (400 characters maximum) and the selected lesson excerpts are sent through Vercel AI Gateway to a model provider. No player identifier or saved learner record is included, and we do not save the conversation. The gateway and provider may keep requests under their own policies.

Service providers

We rely on Vercel (hosting, functions, analytics and AI Gateway) and Upstash (database). If you turn on turn alerts, your browser's push service delivers them. Wallet extensions such as Kasware, and external sites we link to such as Kaspacom, have their own policies and are not run by Geek Protocol.

Backups and deletion

Our database is backed up so progress can be restored after a failure; backups may keep data for a limited time after it is changed or removed. You can remove passkeys and receiving addresses from your account at any time. To have your player record deleted, contact us as described below.

Changes and contact

Updates will appear on this page with a new effective date. For privacy questions or deletion requests, send a direct message to @geekonkas on X. Do not post personal information publicly, and never share a seed phrase or private key with anyone.
