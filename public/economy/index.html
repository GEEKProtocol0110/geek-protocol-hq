    70/30 RECYCLE & BURN

    Follow every platform fee.

    The proposed rule sends 70% of eligible Geek platform fees back to reward funding and allocates 30% for burning. Every paid transaction must identify its fee portion. A planned burn is not a confirmed burn.

    Try an example. This calculator does not charge your wallet, set an item price, or save a transaction.

    70% · Reward funding planned70 GEEK
    30% · Burn pending30 GEEK

    Enter positive transaction amounts with up to eight decimal places. The fee must be between zero and the transaction amount.

    Rounding uses the smallest GEEK unit. Reward funding rounds down; burn pending receives the remainder. Fee rules for each purchase or payout type still need to be finalized.
