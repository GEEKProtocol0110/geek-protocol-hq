      Advanced wallet recovery
      Use an address from another wallet

      Your wallet needs a Sign message feature for Kaspa. Sign the exact login message in that wallet, then paste its signature here. Connecting through Kasware is optional.

      If copying is blocked, choose Select entire message, then touch and hold the selected text and choose Copy. Paste the whole message into your wallet’s Kaspa Sign message screen. Return here to paste the signature before the message expires.

      An address alone cannot sign you in. If your wallet cannot sign Kaspa login messages, continue as a guest for now. Exchange deposit and shared-script addresses may need another ownership method.

      Use the same wallet to recover your saved player. Separate guest progress is not merged.
