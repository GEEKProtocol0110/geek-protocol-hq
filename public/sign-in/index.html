    1. Choose your address. Connect a browser wallet or paste your Kaspa address.

    3. Open My HQ. Continue your progress or change your profile name.

    Using KasWare on your phone?

    Wallet unlock with a fingerprint or face is different from signing a Geek login message. Your mobile wallet must support Kaspa Sign message or a compatible browser connection with message signing.
