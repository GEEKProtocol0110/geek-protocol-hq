READ BEFORE YOU START

One attempt. Clear rules.

  1. Weekly Signal has 10 distinct concepts and a five-minute overall limit. Monthly Circuit has 20 and a ten-minute limit. Both cover eight introductory Kaspa topics.
  2. You have 15 seconds per question. Each correct answer earns 100 points, with no speed or streak bonus. Equal scores share the same rank.
  3. Starting uses your attempt for that period. Reloading or recovering your verified identity resumes that attempt; it does not reset its clocks. The overall timer includes answer review.
  4. You can leave and resume, or finish early. Unanswered questions count as zero. Submit before the period closes; an unsubmitted attempt does not rank.
  5. Weeks begin Monday at 00:00 UTC; months begin on the first at 00:00 UTC. Dates on the cards also show your local time.
  6. The public board shows ten completed submissions, with your own rank on your result. Each period has a 5,000-player Alpha capacity. Results are retained for 60 days after closing.

Public web trivia cannot prevent outside help or people using multiple player identities. These standings are community practice, not a proctored assessment.
