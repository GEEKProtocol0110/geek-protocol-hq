# Someone else must be able to continue

Status: operating design; **no successor or second account administrator is appointed here**. The founder chooses trusted people. A public role record does not create account access, transfer ownership or replace appropriate legal arrangements.

| Role | Responsibility | Appointment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder/steward | Mission, priorities, authority and economic policy | Michael Gee |
| Technical maintainer | Releases, tests, architecture, issue triage and continuation records | Durable successor unassigned |
| Content/education lead | Question quality, curriculum and assessment boundaries | Unassigned |
| Repository/archive custodian | Independent source/originals copies and restore checks | Unassigned |
| Hosting/data custodian | Account recovery, monitoring and managed-provider recovery | Unassigned |
| Finance/contract authority | Verified entity authority, budgets, invoices and agreements | Unverified; actual authority must be established |

One person can initially hold multiple roles. Recovery should not depend on one person's phone or memory. Keep named appointments private until the people accept responsibilities and consent to public identification.

## Private custody inventory

Inventory GitHub ownership/permissions, Vercel project/team and billing, database/backup access, domains/DNS/renewals, release-signing material, any future treasury arrangements, institutional agreements and archive destinations. Store access/recovery material in a controlled system. This public repository holds roles and evidence status only.

Verify recovery using each provider's current supported process with the account owner. Do not share passwords in issues or disable MFA to simplify handover. A wallet address is not authority to sign for its owner. Existing monetary holds remain in force during succession.

## Handover drill

1. Founder appoints a recipient and scope privately; record accepted responsibilities and contacts.
2. Recipient uses [Start Here](../../START-HERE.md) to identify the release, open PRs, active task, holds and next action without the previous chat.
3. On an isolated checkout, verify dependencies/checks and a source snapshot restore. Record commit and missing instructions.
4. Exercise relevant authorized provider access/recovery. Production data follows the provider-restore procedure; a Git clone is insufficient.
5. Recipient prepares a small reversible documentation PR. Establish review, release and rollback responsibilities at the actual project scope.
6. Record gaps and a retest owner. The drill is complete only after an actual appointed person exercises it.

## Unexpected absence and future stewardship

Continue within already granted authority. Preserve records and essential service; record unavailable authority as a blocker. Do not invent a treasury owner, signatory, partner approval or governance rule. An AI cannot reconstruct private custody or make a legal appointment.

The fifty-year direction needs durable ownership, contributor rights, funding, institutional responsibilities and succession that survive personnel and technology changes. Entity/governance form remains undecided. Record a reviewed choice before describing a company, foundation, DAO or trust as established. Keep exportable records so host, chain and AI-provider changes do not erase the work.
