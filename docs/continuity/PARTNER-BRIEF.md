# Geek Protocol learning refresher — discussion draft

**Proposal for discovery; not an offer, awarded contract or certification.**

## The problem to test

People often complete a course and have few opportunities to revisit what matters. Teachers and public-service trainers need a simple way to offer reviewed practice, show useful feedback and identify material that needs another look.

## Proposed pilot

Choose one instructor-approved objective and a short, accessible lesson. Learners practice without needing a crypto wallet, receive explanations and revisit the material later. The instructor reviews the pilot's retention, usability and workload findings against targets agreed in advance.

## Existing foundation

Geek Protocol HQ is a public learning Alpha with guided Study, saved practice feedback and free learning/game surfaces. At the PR #113 checkpoint, the community Alpha had 500 internally source-checked Kaspa questions, 497 Study concepts and four saved Quest chapters. Consult the [current record](CURRENT-STATE.md) for later changes. A partner's course needs its own suitable content and acceptance. Internal source review is not independent educational certification.

## What the parties would define

The course, expert reviewer, participant group, approved release, data/consent boundaries, accessibility, duration, evaluation method, support and budget. Initial recognition can be nonfinancial; any funded learner incentive requires a separate policy. No platform purchase or token holding should be necessary for the proposed learning pilot.

## What is being sought

An educator, training office or authorized public-service sponsor willing to help define one useful pilot. The first discovery deliverable is an agreed [pilot charter](PILOT-CHARTER.md), followed by a technical/content dry run and a decision about recruitment.

Institutional integrations, agency hosting, prices, contract terms and measured outcomes remain to be established. Contact and outreach are founder-controlled; this draft has not been sent to anyone.
