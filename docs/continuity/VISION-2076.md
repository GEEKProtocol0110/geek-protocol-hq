# Geek Protocol: a fifty-year direction, 2026–2076

**Founder direction, October 15, 2026:** preserve the project so others can continue it; build toward schools, government contracts, public-service refresher learning and military learning, with carefully designed rewards. This is a strategic horizon, not a forecast of contracts, token values or fixed delivery dates.

## The purpose that should survive the technology

Make useful knowledge easier to learn, revisit and apply. Help a learner see progress and find the next step. Let teachers and trainers use reviewed material and evidence of practice. Recognize meaningful effort without letting incentives replace understanding.

Giga is the welcoming heart; A.C.E. is the analytical guide. Entertainment can invite learners in, but access, truthful feedback, content quality and human responsibility must endure changes in wallets, chains, devices and AI providers. Preserve exportable content and records so a future migration does not erase the project's history.

## Four product paths

- **Geek Jr and family learning:** guided, age-appropriate practice with parent/educator involvement. No child needs a wallet to learn.
- **Geek Protocol community:** learning, free games, reviewed contributor content and optional ecosystem features within their actual boundaries.
- **Education and professional learning:** an institution chooses objectives and instructors; Geek supplies practice, feedback and useful records.
- **Public-service and military/veteran learning:** agency-approved public refresher content, professional development and transition skills. A sponsor defines scope and acceptance before a contract or integration is promised.

## Rewards and a sustainable business

Institutional subscriptions, content services, support, implementation and evaluation work are proposed revenue paths to test. A training buyer can pay for a useful service without requiring learners to buy a token. Prices, market size, contract awards and margins are not assumed.

First pilots can recognize participation with visible progress and nonfinancial badges. Later financial or token incentives need a funded budget, lawful institutional rules, eligibility, caps, correction/appeal handling and reconciliation. Completion of practice is not automatically professional competence, an official qualification or an entitlement to payment. Contributor rewards and learner incentives need separate policies.

## Review rhythm

The first rolling ninety-day cycle is a planning proposal, with content throughput and appointments still unassigned:

| Window | Focus | Useful exit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weeks 1–2 | Merge continuation records; choose archive/account custodians; preserve supplied originals; finish the small-device pilot; continue the next reviewed content batch. | A tested pickup path, an independent source copy, actual acceptance results and item-level review evidence. |
| Weeks 3–6 | Continue all-category content review; measure editorial throughput; define one educator/public-service discovery problem. | A realistic content capacity plan, corrected bank records and a draft pilot charter with an interested authorized sponsor if available. |
| Weeks 7–13 | Reassess bank progress and recovery gaps; run a synthetic-data course dry run only if the sponsor and scope are ready. | Reviewed go/revise/stop decisions, measured delivery cost and a specific next quarter's backlog. No promise that all banks or an institutional pilot finish within ninety days. |

Use a rolling ninety-day delivery plan for actual work. Review the one-year plan quarterly, the five-year direction annually, and the fifty-year purpose when leadership or evidence changes. Record the decision, reason, owner and changed acceptance criteria in the decision register. These are operating practices, not scheduled jobs created by this change.

Assess retention, accessibility, learner completion, instructor workload, support burden, cost per completed course, content corrections and repeat institutional use. Set targets before each pilot; publish results with limitations. Do not turn leaderboard activity, wallet connections or token price into evidence of learning.
