# Schools, public service and military learning

Status: **proposed delivery path**. No school integration, government contract, military accreditation, institutional deployment or sponsored reward program is verified. This plan gives the founder's ambition a practical sequence; it does not register a business, contact a buyer or collect learner data.

## Start with a useful refresher

A proposed first course is a five-to-ten-minute learning session on an instructor-approved, low-risk topic, followed by a short revisit. Candidate themes include digital literacy, public-service orientation, approved process updates and professional or veteran transition learning. The sponsor selects the subject and expert reviewer. Safety-critical qualification stays with the responsible institution and its qualified assessors.

Use the [pilot charter](PILOT-CHARTER.md) to agree on scope, users, data, budget, owner, success measures and stop conditions. Test an accessible lesson and saved feedback first. Use synthetic or non-identifying data for technical trials; any real learner information needs the specific institution's agreement and reviewed handling plan.

## School path

| Step | Proposed work | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1: educator discovery | Choose learner age/reading level and one curriculum objective; separate family/home learning from a school-managed course. | Educator-approved objective and reviewed material; no invented district partner. |
| E2: bounded pilot | Define instructor/parent roles, minimal learner information, access, retention, deletion, support and accessibility. | Written pilot/data arrangements and completed usable learning flow. |
| E3: evaluation | Measure baseline knowledge, immediate practice and delayed retention, plus usability and instructor workload. | De-identified findings; limitations and corrections; permission for any public case study. |
| E4: integration | Implement only the LMS workflow the pilot actually needs, with institutional identity and isolated roles. | Synthetic-data integration tests, institution acceptance and documented operational ownership. |
| E5: repeatable offering | Specify support, content maintenance, pricing and renewal criteria. | Reliable delivery, useful outcomes and costs that can be funded without speculative assumptions. |

The Department of Education provides FERPA/vendor guidance; the FTC maintains COPPA guidance. Review the current rules with the institution for the actual ages, operator role, data and use. Do not label the Alpha compliant because this plan names a law. No student identity, grades or education records belong in the public Git repository.

Candidate standards have different jobs: LTI connects a tool to a learning environment; QTI concerns assessment interchange; OneRoster concerns roster/course/grade exchanges; xAPI concerns learning-activity communication. These are candidates to evaluate, not implemented HQ integrations or conformance claims. Begin with the smallest agreed export; avoid collecting roster data merely to demonstrate a standard.

## Government and public-service path

1. **Establish buyer fit.** Identify one refresher problem, an authorized sponsor and how success would be accepted. Use the [partner brief](PARTNER-BRIEF.md), marked as a proposal. No outreach is sent by this repository change.
2. **Establish supplier facts.** Privately verify the actual contracting entity, authority, ownership, registration status, budget, insurance/support needs and who may sign. Prepare a factual capability statement; no invented past performance, certifications or staff.
3. **Choose the procurement route.** Evaluate a local/state pilot, a suitable subcontract or a federal prime opportunity with a qualified advisor. Michigan's official SIGMA VSS and SBA/APEX resources are starting points. SAM.gov distinguishes full registration for prime federal awards from a UEI-only record; a UEI does not itself create award eligibility.
4. **Meet the specific solicitation.** Review accessibility evidence, security, hosting, data rights, privacy, records, payment and assessment requirements with the buyer before offering a service. A Section 508 Accessibility Conformance Report describes evaluated accessibility; it is not obtained by writing the words “508 compliant.”
5. **Deliver a bounded paid service.** Define milestones, acceptance, invoices, content corrections and support. Keep agency payment for a service separate from a learner reward policy. Publish a case study only with permission and evidence.

No procurement threshold, tax treatment, bidder eligibility or contract clause is assumed here. The [official sources](SOURCES.md) are a research starting point checked October 15, 2026; requirements must be rechecked for the actual engagement.

## Military and veteran path

Begin with public, non-sensitive professional development, general process refreshers or transition learning. An authorized institutional sponsor chooses approved content, assessment authority and acceptable delivery. xAPI/ADL references can inform later interoperability discovery; they do not establish a Department of Defense relationship or certification.

Before any military institutional deployment, determine the actual data classification, personnel-data rules, hosting/security requirements, procurement route and allowed incentives. The public Alpha has no authority to receive controlled operational material or serve as an official qualification system. Such a requirement would need its own scoped architecture and reviewed implementation; it is not a minor configuration toggle.

## Reward design

Proposed first-pilot recognition is nonfinancial progress and completion badges. A future institution-funded incentive needs an explicit purpose, eligible population, funding source, amount/caps, evidence of qualifying learning, anti-abuse controls, appeals and payment reconciliation. Check employee/service-member gift, compensation, award and program rules for that engagement before offering value. Token use is optional to evaluate, and does not determine access to learning or assessment results.
