## Evaluation record

Record the intended assessment separately from app activity. Define how the revisit is timed, how missing responses are treated and how participants are compared with their baseline. Use suitable instructor-reviewed items. If no comparison group exists, do not attribute improvement solely to Geek Protocol. Small cohorts and volunteer selection limit generalization.

Report completed/started counts, item corrections, delayed-retention results, accessibility issues, privacy incidents if any, and delivery/support cost. Settle whether to stop, revise, repeat or expand. Practice badges do not establish an official qualification.

## Technical acceptance

Use synthetic-data dry runs before collecting real learner information. Verify role boundaries, consent/authority, accessibility, saved progress, export/deletion, outage behavior and recovery at the agreed scope. A successful local test or another cohort's consent is not acceptance for this institution.

## Closeout

Record exact release, dates, actual costs, observed results, limitations, corrections, institutional acceptance and data disposition. Link the approved public summary from the release/backlog record; keep private agreements and learner records in the designated controlled system.
