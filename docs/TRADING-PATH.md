# GEEK trading path

HQ's `/trade/` page routes GEEK buyers and sellers to the existing Kaspacom market at `https://kaspa.com/tokens/marketplace/token/GEEK`. The founder chose this route over building order placement inside HQ.

The page links from Home and Mint and appears in the public sitemap. Buy and Sell both open the verified GEEK market page; the user selects the matching tab on Kaspacom. No undocumented tab parameter, embedded order widget, price feed, or affiliate parameter is added.

## Verified destination

On October 11, 2026, the public GEEK page loaded with the title “GEEK Token — Price, Charts & Trading | KaspaCom”, GEEK token information, and Buy, Sell, and Mint panels. The Buy panel required a wallet connection. This verifies the destination and visible market interface, not available liquidity or a completed trade.

The [Kaspacom buy/sell guide](https://kaspacom.gitbook.io/kaspacom/krc-20-tokens/markdown) describes choosing an order in Buy and setting quantity and a KAS price in Sell. HQ does not repeat its speed or fee guarantees; users review current fees and final cost on the destination and in their wallets.

## Boundaries

- The HQ page is a static guide and external link. It makes no wallet calls and places no orders.
- Wallet connection, listing approval, order execution and settlement remain on Kaspacom. No transaction was initiated during verification.
- HQ does not quote a live GEEK price, fabricate market data, guarantee liquidity, imply a partnership, or enable its own settlement path.
- The guide distinguishes wallet-held GEEK KRC-20 tokens from HQ Alpha credits, XP, and off-chain collectibles. It also distinguishes mint protocol fees from a seller's market price and fees.
