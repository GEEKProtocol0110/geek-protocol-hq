# Brand and Communications Guide

**Document:** GEEK-BRAND-001 · **Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** Founder review draft — not adopted

**Source baseline:** HQ commit `b7521c6db2087d0c40580afa443104f33f4cd212` (November 16, 2026 snapshot). This draft distinguishes recorded implementation from proposed requirements. It does not revise the published whitepaper or activate a release gate.

## Project identity

Use **Geek Protocol** in prose, **GEEK Protocol** where the established wordmark fits, **GEEK** for the token ticker and **$GEEK** in appropriate social contexts. The motto is **All Hope, No Hype.** The project is an independent Kaspa community learning and gaming project.

Suggested factual description: “Geek Protocol helps people learn through guided lessons, quizzes and games, with application records of progress. Its GEEK token is KRC-20 on Kaspa; public Alpha credits and XP are separate from wallet tokens.”

Use “Proof of Learning” for the described application method. Avoid presenting it as Kaspa consensus, an accredited credential or proof that a learner worked without assistance.

## Character identities and original assets

**GIGA** is the established gold robot mascot and welcoming community voice. Use the founder's existing artwork when that identity is required.

**A.C.E.** represents teaching and analysis. The located original archive depicts a blue digital humanoid in [A.C.E. corrupted-code artwork](https://www.geekprotocol.xyz/collection/archive/legacy-art/ace-corrupted-code.webp). This archive reference identifies the character; locating it does not mean the image has been integrated into the Arena.

Authored A.C.E. learning guidance and bounded local game algorithms are distinct from a remote model tutor. Seven board-game opponents currently use local algorithms, without a claimed rating. Optional remote tutor activation remains paused outside that scope.

Do not silently replace either character with a newly generated mascot or declare an archived concept a minted NFT. Preserve source originals and provenance before making derivatives. Public availability does not establish a new reuse license.

## Proposed visual direction

Continue the established dark Geek setting with crisp Kaspa-inspired teal, gold highlights, restrained glows, glass panels and recognizable existing art. Glow should frame an element while its letter shapes remain sharp.

Body text needs readable weight and contrast. Avoid heavy blurred white text, decoration over board controls and forced motion. Use existing design tokens from each product rather than inventing new color specifications in this guide.

Screenshots and videos should show the actual current build. Keep game controls visible on a phone, use purposeful cuts, caption material changes and distinguish mockups from working features. Claiming identical behavior across HQ, Jr and Mini requires evidence from each.

## Language and claim checks

| Subject | Supported wording | Evidence needed for a stronger claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learning | Practice, explanations and recorded progress | A defined, measured study for learning improvement |
| Brain games | Strategy and pattern practice | Specific research before any cognitive benefit claim |
| Token economy | GEEK exists; Alpha records are separate; payouts disabled | Accepted gates and actual settlement evidence |
| Security | Named controls and dated internal checks | Exact-scope independent assessment |
| Collectibles | Planned art structure and off-chain cosmetics | Approved ownership standard and delivery evidence |
| Partnerships | A linked resource or stated proposal | Permission and actual agreement before endorsement claims |

Avoid guaranteed returns, earnings promises, “fully secure,” “audited” without its report, unverified first/only claims, and school or government adoption language without evidence. Do not use token balances or XP as measures of intelligence.
